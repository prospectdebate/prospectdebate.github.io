Our Mission

Why we compete.

Mission Statement

Prospect Speech & Debate exists to develop students into confident, well-researched, and persuasive communicators — people who can walk into any room and command it. We compete not just to win, but to build the habits of mind that last a lifetime.

We believe that the ability to argue clearly and listen critically is one of the most valuable skills a person can develop. Every drill, every tournament, every round exists to sharpen that skill.

About the Program

Built for thinkers who want to compete.

Prospect Speech & Debate is a competitive academic program affiliated with the National Speech and Debate Association. We train students across all experience levels and compete at local, regional, and national tournaments.

Whether you've never given a speech or you've competed before, this program meets you where you are and builds you into someone formidable.

What We Compete In

Two formats. Six events.

Debate
Parliamentary Debate
Fast-paced team debate on a topic released 20 minutes before the round. Pure argumentation, quick thinking, and case-building under pressure.
Speech
Original Oratory
A fully written, memorized persuasive speech on any topic of your choosing. The premier platform event — rewards originality, research, and commanding delivery.
Speech
Dramatic Interpretation
A solo performance cut from published literature — no props, no costumes, just physicality, voice, and emotional truth.
Speech
Humorous Interpretation
A comedic performance from published works. Timing, character control, and the ability to make a room genuinely laugh.
Speech
Extemporaneous Speaking
Draw a current events question. Thirty minutes to prep. Seven minutes to deliver. For students who read the news and love to argue it.
Speech
Impromptu Speaking
A prompt. Two minutes of prep. Five minutes of speech. The most instinct-driven event in the format.
Where We Compete

Local, regional, and national.

We compete across the full competitive calendar — from one-day local invitationals to multi-day national championships. New members are eligible for their first tournament within weeks of joining.

Local
League Invitationals
One-day tournaments hosted by schools in our area. These are where members earn their first competitive experience and build tournament habits. We are part of the CFL League.
Regional
District Championships
Multi-round competitions across the district that determine qualification to state and national-level events. The mid-season benchmark.
Invitational
Scrimmages & Invitationals
Practice tournaments and cross-school scrimmages throughout the year to sharpen skills between major competitions.
