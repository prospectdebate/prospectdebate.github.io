About

A program built on preparation and presence.

Prospect Speech & Debate trains students to research, argue, and speak with precision. We compete locally, regionally, and at national tournaments through the National Speech and Debate Association.

Whether you've never given a speech or you've competed before, this program meets you where you are and pushes you further than you expected.

What We Compete In

Three formats. Nine events.

Debate
Parliamentary Debate
Fast-paced team debate. Topic released 20 minutes before the round. Pure argumentation under pressure.
Speech
Original Oratory
A fully written, memorized persuasive speech on any topic of your choosing.
Speech
Dramatic Interpretation
A solo performance cut from published literature — no props, just physicality and voice.
Speech
Extemporaneous Speaking
Draw a current events question. Thirty minutes to prep. Seven minutes to deliver.
Speech
Impromptu Speaking
A prompt. Two minutes of prep. Five minutes of speech. The most instinct-driven event in the format.
