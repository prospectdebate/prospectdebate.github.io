# Prospect Debate — Brand Kit

## 1. Brand Name
**Primary:** Prospect Debate
**Formal / alt form:** Prospect Parliamentary — use for tournament programs, letterhead, judge-facing materials, or anywhere a more official register is useful. Primary web and social identity stays "Prospect Debate."

## 2. Slogan
**"All we need is 20 minutes."**
Said emphatically, almost as a dare. It's the parli format itself (prep time) turned into a statement of confidence — small window, big impact. Works as a hero headline, a section kicker, or a closing CTA line ("Give us 20 minutes.").

## 3. Brand Positioning
Prospect Debate is a school parliamentary debate club in the middle of a deliberate rebuild — not a legacy powerhouse coasting on reputation, but a program actively "on the rise" and putting that story front and center. The site's job is to do reputation-building work: turn a front-facing, visually striking presence into new members, more respect from other schools/judges at tournaments, and renewed support from parents and the school itself.

Position it as the underdog-turned-contender: honest about the rebuild, confident about the trajectory. "Watch the rise" is the narrative spine, not a marketing gloss over a fully mature program.

## 4. Target Audience
- Prospective members at our own school (the primary audience)
- Students at other schools encountered at tournaments
- Parents evaluating whether this is worth their kid's time
- Judges, coaches, and opposing teams forming an impression of the program
- Anyone reinforcing (or reconsidering) the club's reputation

## 5. Brand Personality
Professional, but jagged — credible enough to be taken seriously by judges and schools, with a fractured, kinetic edge that signals this isn't a sleepy academic club. Think: composed under pressure, but visually unafraid to break its own grid. Confident, a little defiant, on the way up.

## 6. Color Palette
Blue and gold as the foundation, kept restrained and noir-dark so the gold reads as a highlight, not a birthday-party accent. A glitch/chromatic-aberration pair is reserved strictly for transition and motion moments — never used as flat fill color.

## 7. Typography Direction
All available on Google Fonts.

- **Display / headlines:** [Big Shoulders Display](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Big+Shoulders+Display) — condensed, industrial, slightly severe. Gives headlines the "jagged" edge without sacrificing legibility. Use at heavy/black weights for hero statements.
- **Body / UI text:** [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) — clean, professional, highly legible at small sizes. Carries the "credible to judges and parents" half of the personality.
- **Accent / data-readout moments:** [Space Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Mono) — for stat callouts, tournament records, countdown/prep-timer motifs, or any place we want a glitch-terminal feel (e.g. "20:00" prep clock).

## 8. Logo Direction
A panther as the central mark — not a soft mascot illustration, but an angular, faceted panther head or full-body prowl silhouette built from sharp, low-poly shards, rendered as if machined from brushed gold. The facets themselves carry the "jagged" personality; the mark reads as premium and precise rather than gimmicky. Gold line/fill on a noir navy or black ground; must work as a single-color mark (gold-on-black or black-on-white) for jerseys, tournament badges, and favicons. Original silhouette — no resemblance to any existing sports/university panther logos.

## 9. Product Description ("The Product Is the Program")
The offering is the club itself: a competitive parliamentary debate program built from the ground up. Parli debate means minimal prep — two sides, a motion, 20 minutes to construct and deliver a case, no cards or pre-written blocks to hide behind. That format is the whole pitch: it rewards people who can think fast, argue cleanly, and hold a room, and it's learnable by anyone willing to show up. The current chapter is a rebuild — small right now, deliberately visible about getting bigger — so "joining" reads as getting in on the ground floor of something climbing, not slotting into an established machine.

## 10. Key Benefits
- **Critical thinking under pressure** — 20 minutes to build an argument from nothing
- **Research and argumentation skill** that transfers directly to academics and college applications
- **Real teamwork** — parli is partner-based; you sink or rise with someone else
- **Public speaking and composure** that most students never get pushed to build
- **A tournament circuit** — travel, competition, meeting people from other schools
- **Ground-floor ownership** — early members shape a program that's visibly on the way up

## 11. Visual Mood
Lead with **noir**: near-black navy grounds, single-source dramatic lighting, high contrast, generous negative space — a serious, cinematic register that reads as credible rather than gimmicky. The faceted **gold panther is the one loud element**; it gets the light, the reflections, and the center of the frame, while everything around it stays dark, still, and disciplined so the mark carries every scene. Motion is restrained and premium — slow reveals, soft parallax, light catching the facets — not flashy. Any digital edge (a quick shard-settle on the hero, a cyan glint on hover) is used once and sparingly, as punctuation, then it's back to noir stillness.

## 12. Website Goal
The landing page is the recruiting and reputation tool: introduce the rebuild story, make the case for what a student personally gains from joining, and convert interest into action (sign up / contact / show up to a meeting). It needs to look good enough that a kid, a parent, and a rival team's coach all come away with the same impression — this program is serious and it's rising. Visual impact is the priority; everything else is built to support it.

## 13. Suggested Landing Page Sections
1. **Hero** — full-bleed noir background, the faceted gold panther holds center frame under a single dramatic light, slogan lands: "All we need is 20 minutes."
2. **The Rebuild** — short, honest framing of where the club is and where it's headed ("watch the rise")
3. **What You Gain** — card/grid section: critical thinking, research, teamwork, public speaking, etc. (ties to Key Benefits)
4. **How It Works** — quick explainer of parliamentary format, using the 20-minute prep clock as a visual motif
5. **Results / Momentum** — pull from `history.html`; a teaser of standings, medals, growth so far
6. **Tournament Calendar** — teaser pulling from `calendar.html`, linking to the full schedule
7. **Team / Culture** — photos, a sense of who's actually in the room
8. **Join / Contact CTA** — clear next step for prospective members, parents, and other schools

## 14. Hero Motion Brief (optional, future asset)
If/when we generate a hero background video or animation, brief for that asset:

A noir-dark studio void — near-black navy gradient, single hard rim light from above-left. Slow push-in camera on the faceted brushed-gold panther prowling in place, light traveling across the facets and igniting the edges as the camera moves. A single, restrained shard-settle at the start (a few loose facets locking into place) is the only "effect" — no chromatic glitch, no scan lines — then clean, premium stillness. Holds on the panther as "All we need is 20 minutes." types on in Space Mono beneath it, terminal-style. Duration: 4–6 seconds, loopable.
