The Program

How a round
works.

Parliamentary debate is a two-person event. You get a motion and twenty minutes to prepare, then you argue it in front of a judge — no scripts and no evidence files.

What You Gain

What you get
out of it.

A few things debate builds that carry over to school and beyond.

01

Think On Your Feet

You build a case in twenty minutes on a topic you just heard. It gets easier to organize your thoughts quickly.

02

Speak Clearly

Presenting and holding a room is a skill you practice — not something you're born with.

03

Work With A Partner

Parli is two people. You prep, win, and lose together.

04

Follow The World

You end up keeping up with current events, economics, and policy to build arguments.

The Format

Minute by minute.

A new motion every round, built in the prep room in the minutes before you speak.

T–20:00

The motion drops

A topic is announced — policy, values, current events. You've never seen it before. Neither has the other team.

T–19:59

Prep starts

Twenty minutes with your partner to define the debate, build your case, and predict everything the other side will say.

T–00:00

You walk in

Forty minutes of speeches. No notes handed to you, no scripts. The judge votes on who argued better — that's the whole game.
